Part 1 User Acquisition: How to Convince Students to Sign In

College students sign up when driven by three psychological triggers: Curiosity, Campus FOMO, and Safety/Anonymity.

👀 1. Blurred Teaser Deck (Live on Landing)

Unauthenticated visitors land directly on a live teaser feed showing 50+ students from CS, Mechanical, and Electrical swiping. Profile photos are styled with frosted-glass blur. Swiping or tapping unblur prompts: "Sign in with YMCA student ID to see who's waiting for you!"

💌 2. Secret Campus Crush Loop

A student inputs their crush's name/handle. An automated anonymous email or alert triggers: "A student from YMCA added you as their Secret Crush! Sign in to match." One active user brings 5 to 10 peers to the app organically.

🔥 3. Snapchat-Style Campus Streaks

Chat streaks (🔥 1d, 3d, 7d) make students return every single day. Streak levels (Maggi Buddies to Canteen Partners) trigger social pride and prevent churn.

🔒 4. 100% Verified Safety Guarantee

Female students and shy introverts need privacy guarantees. Highlights on posters: "100% Genuine YMCA IDs Only • No Creeps • Blurred Photo Mode Until Mutual Match."
